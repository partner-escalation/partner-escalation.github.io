    How these numbers are calculated

    Open
    Status is anything except Resolved or Rejected.
    New / Pending
    Status is New or Reopened (nobody has picked it up yet).
    Overdue
    Open, and more than hours since the complaint was submitted (SLA_HOURS in Payment Policy).
    Resolution time
    Resolved timestamp minus submitted timestamp, for complaints whose current status is Resolved. Unresolved and Rejected complaints are excluded from averages. Reopening clears the resolved timestamp, so the complaint counts again only when it is resolved again (measured from the original submission).
    Staff attribution
    Each complaint counts for its current assigned admin. Resolution rate = resolved ÷ assigned.
    Date range
    Filters by submission date. Daily/monthly "Resolved" counts use the resolved date.

    How it works

    Auto-reply
    Sent to the partner the moment they submit. The most specific match wins: a template for the exact subcategory beats one for the whole category, which beats "Any". A "No proof attached" template is used only when the partner attached no file.
    Quick reply
    Shown in each complaint's Actions card. One click fills the partner message, the new status and the resolution; you can edit before saving.
    Auto-close
    Every hour, complaints in "Waiting for Partner" with no update for AUTO_CLOSE_DAYS days are closed with this message. Set the days (0 = off) in Payment Policy.
    Placeholders
    Words in {curly brackets} are filled in automatically, for example {name}, {complaintId} and {visitCharge}. Amounts come from Payment Policy, so the replies stay correct when the policy changes.

    Automatic messages only repeat the configured policy. They do not check payout records, so they never confirm that money was paid.
